About Justin

I was born in Beauceville, Québec, on June 9, 2004. From a young age, discipline and curiosity defined how I moved through the world — whether captaining a competitive hockey team, building bathrooms in Ecuador, or performing at an international theatre competition in Russia.

My pivot into finance was concrete, not theoretical. A Real Estate Economics course at Monash University in Australia turned investing from an interest into a true direction. I built my first full financial model there, then road-tripped across the continent — coast to desert — returning home more focused, resilient, and intentional than I'd ever been.

Today I'm completing a double B.B.A. in Finance & Management at Bishop's University (graduating in fall 2026) while working at BMO: Bank Teller from October 2025, Personal Banker Assistant since May 2026, the month I earned the Investment Funds in Canada (IFIC) certification. In September 2026 I began TELUQ's Financial Planning Certificate, on the path to the Pl. Fin. title, and in January 2027 I start an MBA in Urban & Real Estate Management at Université Laval.

Construction JL Groleau is my family's business — construction, real estate and development in Beauce. I support it with project valuation, AI integration and operational improvements, because I believe technical credentials and real-world judgment need to grow together.

Beyond my path, M&A, LBOs and artificial intelligence are the subjects I keep coming back to — AI especially, applied to finance and to building tools like this portfolio and the BMO Fund Explorer. I always welcome advice from finance professionals; if you have a recommendation, I would genuinely love to hear it.
